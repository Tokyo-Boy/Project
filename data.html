Data Collection Process

What data or numbers has been or will be collected to measure effectiveness?

We can monitor visitors to the site and encourage them to reach out with questions, further ideas, and stories of how they are helping to avoid food waste. They can contribute their own ideas. Visitor count is one way to do this since the project is education based.

How will the data be presented? (a chart, table, etc)

The website will collect charts and tables as part of the education process, and a chart can also show the visitors to the website and any feedback we collect. The data can continue to grow long after the end of the project.
